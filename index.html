How Kairo sorts your time

Changes apply to past and future data. A browser's own category only counts for sites Kairo can't recognise.

Settings

Daily goals

Progress shows on Today, and Kairo lets you know when you reach a target or get close to a limit. Limits for single apps and sites are set in Categories.

Privacy

Everything stays on this computer. Private browser windows are recorded without titles or sites, and password managers aren't recorded at all. Choose what's recorded for each app or site in Categories.

Browser extension

Without it, Kairo guesses sites from window titles, which misses most pages in Brave and Chrome. The extension tells Kairo the exact site instead. It sends only the domain, never the page or what you type, and only to Kairo on this computer.

  1. Pick your browser below. Kairo opens its extensions page and copies the extension folder's location.
  2. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and paste the location (Ctrl+V) into the folder box.

Start with Windows

Kairo can only record while it's running, so this keeps your history complete.

Focus nudges

At most one notification a night, and only while you're at the computer.

Breaks

Only time you're actually at the computer counts, and 5 minutes away resets it. Reminders wait until a focus session or a game is over.

Demo data

Load three weeks of made-up activity to try the insights now. It's flagged separately and can be cleared without touching your real history.

Updates

Kairo looks for a new version a minute after it starts and every 6 hours, and installs it quietly.
