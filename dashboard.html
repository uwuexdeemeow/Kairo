Your data

Take your tracked time into a spreadsheet, or keep everything in one backup file. A backup restores on any computer, Windows, Mac or Linux, so it's also how you move Kairo to a new one.

Combine computers

See the time from all your computers together. Link them with a short code: your time travels through Kairo's relay, encrypted end to end, so the relay can't read it. Or pick a folder your cloud drive already syncs (OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive, Syncthing) on each computer, and your time, including window titles, goes to that drive instead. Categories, limits, goals and blocking rules you change are shared too, the latest change winning everywhere.

    Type this on your other computer. It works once, for 10 minutes.

    Start over? Every computer linked now stops combining with this one, including one that's lost or sold. Link the ones you still use again with a new code.
