      Your commands

      A word you choose that does something when you type it in the palette: opens a link, opens a file, folder or app, or copies some text. Links and text go to the computers you've combined; files, folders and apps stay on this one.

      App ring

      Open it with

      A dial of Kairo's apps at the pointer.

      How to turn it

      Keep holding the first key and press the last one again to turn it, then let go to open. Or tap the shortcut and turn it with the arrows, the wheel or by dragging.

      What's on it

      Capture and scanning

      Capture

      Keeps what you've copied in Ledger from any window: a short line as a task, anything longer as a note. During a focus session on a task, the note goes on that task. Kairo only reads what's already on the clipboard; it never copies for you.

      Capture with

      Set keys to capture from any window. Without them, type capture in the palette.

      Then “Call Leo tomorrow !high” is due tomorrow, at high priority. Off, copied words are kept just as they are.

      VirusTotal

      Check files, links and hashes from the palette: type scan or vt. A file is looked up by its fingerprint; it's uploaded only if you choose to.

      Kept on this computer only, never synced. Free from virustotal.com → your profile → API key.

      Data and computers

      Your data

      Your time as a spreadsheet, or everything in one backup file. A backup is also how you move Kairo to a new computer, Windows, Mac or Linux.

      Replaces what's on this computer with the backup's. What's here now is saved to a file first.
