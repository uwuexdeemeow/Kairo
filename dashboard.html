Your data

Take your tracked time into a spreadsheet, or keep everything in one backup file. A backup restores on any computer, Windows, Mac or Linux, so it's also how you move Kairo to a new one.

Combine computers

See the time from all your computers together. Pick a folder your cloud drive already syncs (OneDrive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive, Syncthing) on each computer; Chrono keeps its time there every few minutes, and adds in the others'. Your time, including window titles, goes to that drive. Categories, limits, goals and blocking rules you change are shared too, the latest change winning everywhere.

    Updates

    Kairo looks for a new version a minute after it starts and every 6 hours, and installs it quietly.
