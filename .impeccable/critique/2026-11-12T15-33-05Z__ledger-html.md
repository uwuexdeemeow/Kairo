## Design Specificity Verdict
Partly authored, mostly category-standard. Serif titles over a small-caps date, the amber accent, the overdue ribbon and Calendar's today ring are Ledger's. Below the heading it is rounded cards and pill chips; dark has no paper feel. Detector: 1 advisory finding on ledger.html, 0 on the seven Ledger source files, 15 across browser states.

## Priority issues
1. [P1] Task detail panel is a ~20-control form, always open, overlaying the list; Steps and Delete below the fold. Fix: lead with title, due, Mark done, Focus; rest behind "+ Add" chips. Command: distill, then layout.
2. [P1] Task rows pile up undifferentiated metadata (date, project, tags, steps, focus time, play button). Fix: date gutter, one project label, tags collapsed to "+n", play on hover/focus. Command: quieter.
3. [P2] Projects and tags look the same; filter bar mixes seven equal choices; pressed chip subtle. Fix: label groups, accent fill when pressed. Command: clarify.
4. [P2] Contrast and tiny labels: #count-tasks 3.9:1, selected row's day label 4.3:1, four 10px group headers (below 11px), "← Kairo" button default black text. Command: polish (typeset for sizes).
5. [P2] The ledger idea stops at the heading; body is generic cards. Operate surface: answer with restraint (hairline-ruled entries, date gutter), not decoration. Command: bolder, carefully.
